# Third-party data

The benchmark files in `data/raw/` are public research datasets. Keep their original citations and licenses in any paper or redistribution.

| Files | Source | Citation / license |
|---|---|---|
| `ETTh1.csv`, `ETTh2.csv`, `ETTm1.csv`, `ETTm2.csv` | [ETDataset](https://github.com/zhouhaoyi/ETDataset) | Zhou et al., Informer, AAAI 2021; follow the repository terms. |
| `Weather.csv`, `Electricity.csv`, `Traffic.csv`, `Exchange.csv` | [THUML Time-Series-Library dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/thuml/Time-Series-Library) | THUML dataset repository, CC BY 4.0; cite the associated benchmark work. |

The download URLs and filenames are recorded in `ts_rsi/data/catalog.py` so that dataset provenance is reproducible.
